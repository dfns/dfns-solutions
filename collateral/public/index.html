1 · Deposit and accept funds

Send Sepolia ETH to the vault address above from any wallet or faucet. Everything that reaches the vault, including the PYUSD payout, lands as Quarantined and must be accepted before it counts as Available. Keep some ETH unpledged: the vault pays its own gas.

2 · Request a loan

Pledge Available ETH and borrow PYUSD. The lender locks the collateral in your vault. You can't move it while the lock is live, and the lender can only send it to a fixed beneficiary address.
