Setup

1
Deploy StableCoin
Deploy an ERC-20 stablecoin (EURC) used as the settlement currency for the bond
2
Mint EURC
Mint stablecoins to both wallets — the investor needs funds to subscribe, and the issuer needs funds to pay coupons later
3
Deploy Bond
Deploy the bond contract with face value, interest rate, coupon schedule, and maturity. APR is in basis points (400 = 4%)

Lifecycle

5
Close Issuance
Lock the subscription book and start the interest accrual clock. No more investors can subscribe after this
6
Withdraw Proceeds
Transfer the EURC raised from subscriptions to the issuer's wallet
8
Deposit Coupon
Fund the next coupon period by depositing the required interest payment into the bond contract
10
Return Principal
Deposit EURC back into the bond contract so investors can redeem their bonds at maturity

Actions

4
Subscribe
Approve the bond contract to spend EURC, then deposit into the subscription escrow. The amount must be a multiple of the bond's face value
7
Claim Bond Tokens
Mint ERC-20 bond tokens proportional to the subscription amount. Can only be done after issuance is closed
9
Claim Coupon
Collect your pro-rata share of the coupon interest payment. The coupon must be funded and its date must have passed. The suggested index below is the most recently funded coupon (Next Unfunded Coupon # − 1) — override it if you need to claim a different one
11
Redeem
Burn your bond tokens and receive the face value in EURC. Only available after maturity and once the issuer has returned the principal
